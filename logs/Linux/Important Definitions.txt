Important Definitions:

PID		each process will have an ID associated with it, also known as its PID. The PID increments for the order In which the process starts.
systemd 	is one of the first processes that are started.
SIGTERM - 	Kill the process, but allow it to do some cleanup tasks beforehand
SIGKILL - 	Kill the process - doesn't do any cleanup after the fact
SIGSTOP - 	Stop/suspend a process
